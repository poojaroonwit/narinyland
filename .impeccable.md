## Design Context

### Users
Narinyland is used by couples who want a private, romantic shared space for memories, pets, letters, rewards, and a growing 3D world. The interface should support playful daily visits, emotional reflection, and light customization without feeling like a productivity tool.

### Brand Personality
Soft, romantic, detailed, peaceful. The app should feel like a cozy realistic garden game: warm, intimate, tactile, and calm, with gentle magic reserved for love-tree growth, pets, memories, and sky moments.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a cozy realistic garden-game direction. Prioritize natural materials, soft daylight, golden-hour warmth, detailed terrain, smooth camera movement, and peaceful world-building controls. Avoid harsh neon, loud arcade styling, generic sci-fi effects, and overly busy fantasy visuals.

### Design Principles
- Make the land feel inhabitable before making it flashy.
- Let customization feel like tending a garden: calm, direct, and forgiving.
- Keep romantic magic subtle and emotionally tied to the couple's actions.
- Prefer tactile game affordances for 3D controls: explore, place, drag, rotate, snap, and inspect.
- Preserve performance with quality-aware detail levels and graceful reduced-complexity states.
